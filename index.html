Reflection on Attainment

Communication & Presentation

Making a technical argument land with a non-expert audience

The Entrepreneurism pitch and my Work-Based Learning project both demanded the same thing: turning detailed commercial reasoning into something an audience could follow and believe in a few minutes, not a few pages.

Built through leading the positioning and go-to-market sections of the RANG Beauty pitch, preparing for live Q&A on the whole venture, and completing a LinkedIn Learning course on professional speaking.

Teamwork & Collaboration

Changing my own work when the group's feedback was right

Working inside a five-person venture team meant my own section of the plan had to connect to everyone else's, and hold up under challenge from teammates who saw gaps I hadn't.

Built through revising the RANG Beauty marketing plan after teammate feedback, serving as Secretary for the Ravensbourne University Bangladeshi Community, and collaborating within a five-person Work-Based Learning group on a shared destination-marketing strategy.

Commercial & Business Awareness

Reading a market from the shop floor, not just a textbook

Two years in retail gave me a direct view of where customer need and commercial decision-making actually meet — experience I learned to treat as evidence, not just a part-time job.

Built through retail roles at KIKO Milano and Inglot Cosmetics, industry and competitor mapping during PLP22203, and shaping the target segment and pricing logic for RANG Beauty.
